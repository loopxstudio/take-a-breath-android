Take a Breath - Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 (version 0.1.0)

Take a Breath is made by LoopX Studio, Toronto, Canada.

In short: the app collects nothing. Everything stays on your phone.

1. What we collect

We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.

2. What is kept on your phone

The app keeps these settings in its private storage on your device, so it can work:

  • the list of apps you chose to guard;
  • your breathing, session, cooldown and active-window settings, including your saved profiles;
  • the time your current session and cooldown end;
  • if you switch on the parent lock, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your PIN and of your recovery code. The PIN itself is never stored.

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Usage access: lets the app notice which app is in front, so it can show the breathing pause before an app you chose. The app only checks whether that app is one of your guarded apps. It does not read what is inside any app, and it does not keep a history.
  • Display over other apps: lets the breathing pause and the rest screen appear on top of the app you are opening.
  • Notifications and foreground service: Android requires a small running notice while the app watches for your guarded apps.
  • Start at boot: lets the app resume after you restart your phone.
  • Audio focus (no permission needed): while a pause or rest screen is showing, the app asks Android to pause any playing video or music, so it does not keep playing behind the screen.

The app does not record your screen, read your messages, use your camera or microphone, or access your location, contacts or files.

4. Children

Take a Breath is for adults, and for parents and carers who set it up on a child's phone or tablet. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Nothing is sent off the device, and the app has no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking. Parents choose which apps are guarded and when. The optional parent lock uses a PIN that only the parent knows, and it is stored only on the device.

5. Links to other sites

The Parent lock section has a "Learn more" button that opens the Google Family Link web page in your browser. That site is run by Google and has its own privacy policy.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

You can switch the app off, remove guarded apps, or change any setting at any time. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no account or server copy to delete. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version will ship with the app and be published on the store page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support.takeabreath@loopxstudio.ca.
